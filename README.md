Commercial revenue analytics, transaction forecasting, and **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Customer Segmentation** designed to isolate profitable cohorts, optimize discount thresholds, and monitor cross-border margin performance.

---

## 📌 Features
- **Transaction Pipeline**: Gross-to-Net revenue waterfall modeling with discounting rules and unit margin metrics.
- **RFM Customer Segmentation**: Quantile categorization isolating champions, loyalists, at-risk, and dormant customer bands.
- **Enterprise DAX Library**: Pre-built DAX measures for Year-over-Year (YoY) growth, customer lifetime value, and rolling 90-day moving averages.

---
